Build Once, Answer Many

Which sustainability topics a seafood-processing SME should build first — who demands each, and exactly what data to collect

Why go first?

Building data capability ahead of demand beats re-collecting it per request — lower long-run cost, steadier market access (Sharma & Vredenburg, 1998; Torugsa et al., 2013).

↓
Lower cost, not just lower risk. Proactive firms outperform reactive ones financially (Russo & Fouts, 1997; Barney, 1991).
🔑
Market access. Several credentials here are already a precondition for staying listed with large retailers (Chapter 5).
€
Investor access. ESG data increasingly shapes financing terms — better ready than scrambling.
