# Supporting files — full set

*Build Once, Answer Many: Where Disclosure Demand Converges for European Seafood-Processing SMEs*
Linne Verhoeven · supervised by Prof. Jean-Philippe Bonardi · in collaboration with Seafood Europe · August 2026

All ten analysis files behind the thesis, matching the index in **Appendix H**. Every workbook opens on a README, INSTRUCTIONS or Legend sheet.

---

## Chapter 4 — the disclosure ecosystem (Step 1)

**`Necessity_Domain_Matrix_v8.xlsx`** — the 49-instrument catalogue, each classified by necessity tier, domain, stakeholder source and obligation locus. The `Classification` sheet is where Chapter 4's counts come from: 26 Scored + 4 Opt-in = 30 scored, plus 7 Context, 11 Excluded, 1 Baseline.

## Chapter 5 — the cohort frontier (Step 2)

These three chain in one direction:

```
ESRS_KPI_Mapping_V9.xlsx  →  ESRS_Coverage_Analytics_v7.ipynb  →  ESRS_Coverage_Results.xlsx
(raw coding, per firm)       (builds the coverage matrix)          (the results in the chapter)
```

- **`ESRS_KPI_Mapping_V9.xlsx`** — each firm's report coded at disclosure-requirement level. Only `ESRS_MASTER` and `Mapping_ALLStacked_TU_N25` are live; the per-firm sheets and earlier scenarios are kept as audit trail.
- **`ESRS_Coverage_Analytics_v7.ipynb`** — Colab notebook, Cells 1–17. All outputs saved, so every chart is readable without running it.
- **`ESRS_Coverage_Results.xlsx`** — 11 sheets, opens on README. The file Chapter 5 and Appendix G cite.
- **`GRI_ESRS_v6.xlsx`** + **`GRI_ESRS_Mapping_v4.ipynb`** — the GRI→ESRS bridge, needed because three firms report under GRI (Thai Union *in accordance with*; Nomad Foods and Profand *with reference to*). Sheets are grouped by company.

## Chapter 6 — cross-framework demand mapping (Steps 3–4)

- **`Phase3_Coverage_Matrix_v17.xlsx`** — the scored matrix: 32 topic rows × 31 instrument columns = 992 cells, 181 carrying demand, with a per-cell justification log.
- **`GRI_13_Topics_FINAL.xlsx`** — GRI 13's 26 material topics classified against the ESRS as Covered / Reporting gap / Structural gap. Source of the four sector-material rows.
- **`VSME_Basic_and_Comprehensive.xlsx`** — VSME datapoints, used for the proportionate-subset crosswalk.
- **`SME_Datapoint_Guide.xlsx`** — 113 datapoints across 32 topics, each with a plain-language reading and a first collection step. The data layer behind the diagnostic tool.

## Two things worth knowing before you look

**Coding rests on a single analyst's documented judgement.** Every non-zero cell has a rationale and a source clause logged, but there is no second coder. The member-checking round with the six cohort firms has been sent and is outstanding at the time of writing; §3.3 and §7.6 say so explicitly.

**This is the canonical set.** Duplicate working copies, Excel lock files and superseded versions have been removed, so nothing here is stale.
