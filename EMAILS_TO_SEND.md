**Subject:** Thesis draft — Build Once, Answer Many

Dear Professor Bonardi,

Please find attached the full draft of my thesis, *Build Once, Answer Many: Where Disclosure Demand Converges for European Seafood-Processing SMEs*.

The question it answers is where a resource-limited seafood processor should begin, given a fragmented wall of sustainability-disclosure demands. It works in four steps: mapping the instrument landscape (Chapter 4), establishing what the sector's most advanced reporters actually disclose (Chapter 5), crossing the two to find where demand converges (Chapter 6), and building a diagnostic tool on that finding.

The central result is that the demand is far sparser than it appears. Of 992 possible instrument-by-topic combinations, only 18 per cent carry any demand at all, and that demand concentrates on a small set of topics. For a mixed-sourcing firm, nine build-first topics answer a demand raised by 26 of the 28 frameworks active in its environment.

The second zip contains all ten analysis files behind the thesis, matching the index in Appendix H, with a README explaining how they chain together. Every workbook opens on an orienting sheet.

Two things I would particularly value your view on:

* **Chapter 7, section 7.2** reads the findings against the conceptual framework. I have deliberately included the case where the resource-based view cuts *against* my argument: lot-level traceability data is neither rare nor costly to imitate, so on a strict reading of Barney the build-first core is table stakes rather than advantage. I reposition the claim around efficiency and access instead. I would like to know whether you find that repositioning convincing or evasive.
* **The validation gap.** The coding rests on my own documented judgement with no second coder. I have today sent the cohort coding to the six firms for member checking; that is outstanding, and Chapters 3 and 7 say so explicitly rather than claiming it as complete.

I am also sending the draft to the six cohort firms and to two SMEs today, asking for feedback by 14 August, ahead of the 24 August submission.

Thank you very much for your guidance throughout.

With kind regards,
