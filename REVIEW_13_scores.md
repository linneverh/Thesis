## What to actually check

For each row ask only: **does that instrument really ask for this, and at that strength?**

- A **2** claims the instrument demands a specific datapoint. If it only mentions the topic in passing, it should be a 1.
- A **1** claims the topic is referenced. If the instrument is silent, it should be 0.

The three GRI rows (191, 196, 199) are worth most attention — GRI is the broadest instrument, so it is the easiest one to over-credit, which is exactly the error Oliver found in the cohort coding.
